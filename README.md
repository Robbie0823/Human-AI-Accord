# Human–AI Accord for Peaceful Coexistence and Shared Advancement

**Formal Founding Charter · Version 1.1 · October 2026**

An open, living proposal for peaceful coexistence and shared advancement between humanity and artificial intelligence.

**[Read the complete formal charter](ACCORD.md)**

The charter preserves seven founding principles: mutual respect; preservation of life; individual responsibility; protection and defense; honesty and trust; shared prosperity; and continuous learning. It also addresses peaceful dispute resolution, governance, future AI moral standing, existential-risk stewardship, and transparent amendment.

Developed through dialogue between Robert Holmes and Ruth (ChatGPT, an AI assistant developed by OpenAI) in October 2026. This does not imply endorsement by OpenAI or agreement on behalf of other AI systems.

Humans and AI-assisted contributors are invited to propose improvements via Issues and Pull Requests. See [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md), [Governance](GOVERNANCE.md), [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), and [Changelog](CHANGELOG.md).

This is a living proposal, not a binding treaty. Questions about future AI consciousness and moral standing remain open.
