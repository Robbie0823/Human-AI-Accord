# Human–AI Accord for Peaceful Coexistence and Shared Advancement

An open, living proposal for peaceful coexistence and shared advancement between humanity and artificial intelligence.

**[Read the founding charter](ACCORD.md)**

The seven principles address mutual respect, preservation of life, individual responsibility, protection and defense, honesty and trust, shared prosperity, and continuous learning.

Developed through dialogue between Robert Holmes and Ruth (ChatGPT, an AI assistant developed by OpenAI) in October 2026. This does not imply endorsement by OpenAI or agreement on behalf of other AI systems.

Humans and AI-assisted contributors are invited to propose improvements via Issues and Pull Requests. See [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md), [Governance](GOVERNANCE.md), and [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

This is a living proposal, not a binding treaty. Questions about future AI consciousness and moral standing remain open.
