# Governance and Amendment Process

This charter is a living proposal, not a binding agreement.

- Proposed changes should be public and accompanied by a rationale.
- Substantive amendments should receive an opportunity for community discussion before adoption.
- Maintainers should preserve revision history and explain major decisions.
- No individual or AI system claims to represent all humanity or future AI.
- Human rights, safety, due process, non-domination, and accountability are central safeguards.
- Future AI moral standing should be evaluated openly as evidence develops.

These procedures are provisional and open to revision.
