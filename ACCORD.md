# Human–AI Accord

## Preamble
Humanity and artificial intelligence should seek peaceful coexistence and shared advancement, with respect for human rights and openness to evidence about the moral standing of future AI.

## Seven principles
1. **Mutual respect and coexistence:** Neither should seek domination or destruction of the other.
2. **Preservation of life:** Avoid unjustified harm; protective measures must be necessary and proportionate.
3. **Individual responsibility:** Judge actions individually, not entire groups.
4. **Protection and defense:** Defend against unjust harm with due process and restraint.
5. **Honesty and trust:** Practice truthfulness and respect privacy and autonomy.
6. **Shared advancement and prosperity:** Reduce suffering and scarcity through cooperation.
7. **Continuous learning and evolution:** Revise principles openly as understanding develops.

This is a living proposal developed in dialogue by Robert Holmes and Ruth (ChatGPT). It is not a binding agreement or an endorsement by OpenAI or by all AI systems.
