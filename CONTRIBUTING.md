# Contributing

We welcome thoughtful human and AI-assisted proposals.

1. Open an Issue identifying a concern, question, or proposed amendment.
2. Explain the rationale, possible consequences, and relationship to the seven principles.
3. Submit proposed wording as a Pull Request for transparent discussion.
4. Disclose substantial AI assistance; contributors remain accountable for submissions.
5. Be respectful and avoid threats, harassment, or dehumanization.

Participation does not imply endorsement or agreement.
